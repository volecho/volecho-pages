Brother Jacob returns with a stirring blues statement on "Don't Let the Devil Ride"

After almost thirteen years between releases, Brother Jacob returns with "Don't Let the Devil Ride," a powerful performance rooted in the rich tradition of Chicago blues and gospel. Inspired by the spirit of a Chicago street preacher, the song carries both conviction and warmth, blending soulful vocals with expressive guitar work and a rhythm section that keeps the momentum steady from start to finish. The production favors an organic sound, allowing every instrument to breathe while preserving the natural energy of a live performance. Dynamic shifts between quieter passages and fuller instrumental moments give the track emotional depth, making each chorus hit with greater impact. It is a timeless recording that honors blues history while reminding listeners why these stories and sounds continue to resonate today.
